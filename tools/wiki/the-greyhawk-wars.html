The Greyhawk Wars

History · 582–584 CY

The cataclysmic wars of 582 to 584 CY that reshaped the Flanaess more thoroughly than any conflict in living memory. Two great threats moved simultaneously — and it is debated to this day whether that was coordination or coincidence.

In the north, Iuz the Old One launched his long-planned invasion. His armies of humanoids, demons, and undead swept south and east from the Empire of Iuz, shattering the Shield Lands in a single devastating campaign and pushing deep into Furyondy and Veluna. The Shield Lands effectively ceased to exist as a political entity. Tens of thousands died or fled. The northern Flanaess still bears the scars.

In the south, the Scarlet Brotherhood struck simultaneously in a coordinated wave of covert action. Assassins killed rulers. Political agents installed Brotherhood puppets. Nations that had stood for generations collapsed or were transformed within weeks. The southern coast of the Flanaess changed hands with breathtaking speed and very little open battle.

The wars ended in 584 CY with the signing of the Pact of Greyhawk, brokered in the Free City. Iuz refused to participate and was not bound by it. The Pact recognized the new reality on the ground, acknowledged massive territorial losses for many nations, and left deep resentments that festered for years. The Circle of Eight suffered its own disaster during the signing, when the member Rary betrayed the organization and fled.
