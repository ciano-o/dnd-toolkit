Pact of Greyhawk

History · Event · Signed 584 CY

The treaty signed in 584 CY that formally ended the Greyhawk Wars. Representatives from dozens of nations gathered in the Free City of Greyhawk to negotiate peace, codify new borders, and acknowledge the territorial realities that two years of devastating war had carved out.

The negotiations were not clean. Many signatories were ratifying losses they could not reverse rather than agreements they had chosen. Nations that had been shattered during the war sent representatives to sign away lands already occupied by their conquerors. The document is as much a record of defeat as a peace treaty.

Iuz the Old One refused to participate and was not bound by the Pact. His Empire retained all territories seized during the war, and the agreement simply drew new borders around his conquests without his input or acknowledgment. This anomaly was noted and quietly accepted — no signatory was in a position to force his compliance.

The signing was marred by treachery. The archmage Rary, a member of the Circle of Eight, murdered two fellow members — Otiluke and Tenser — during the proceedings, then fled with the warrior Lord Robilar to the Bright Desert. His motives remain disputed. The Circle was left shaken and reduced at the very moment its influence was most needed.

The Free City gained considerable prestige as the neutral ground where peace was forged. The Pact is referenced in nearly every subsequent diplomatic document in the Flanaess.
